# Surface brief: the blog (post page, home, About)

Scope: every page of elizandro.com.br. Visitor mode: Read.

Audience and job: Fabio first, then guests who land on one post from a link or search and want to read it comfortably to the end. Content: long-form posts with Go code, quotations and the occasional image; a short list of posts; one About page. Constraints: Hugo on GitHub Pages, no build step beyond Hugo, one theme, body and code at 7:1 contrast or better.

Memorable moment: the full stop of the "fe." monogram can be picked up and thrown, and turns into a small game of keeping it in the air.

## Direction contract

THESIS: One white sheet of paper lifted off a barely-lavender ground, with soft hills of mist across the top. The writing owns the sheet; personality lives in a full stop. It refuses the centred white developer-blog column with a blue link colour, and it refuses ornament.

OWN-WORLD: A near-white lavender ground; a white sheet with layered shadow; four tints of pale lavender hills at the top of the ground, fading out downward; nothing but tones of violet: near-black violet ink, a deep lavender for links and the monogram's letters, a mid lavender for the full stop wherever it appears, a very pale lavender for code and quotations. Literata for titles and text, Hanken Grotesk for labels, JetBrains Mono for code.

STORY: The visitor arrives on a post, reads without distraction, and leaves knowing this is one person's own place.

FIRST VIEWPORT: On the ground, the monogram alone at the left edge of the sheet and Writing / About at its right edge, the current one marked by a full stop beneath it. Below, the sheet, about 800px wide, with the title left-aligned at about 48px and closed by a lavender full stop, a byline with date and reading time, and the first paragraphs. From laptop widths up a small white card to the right of the sheet lists the post's sections and marks the current one with a full stop.

FORM: user-pinned direction; seed: none (pinned). "let's perhaps use lavender background and a white sheet for the content. Let's work with tons of lavender for other elements"; "the ton for the lavender colour should be even more ligher and subtle"; "I like the use of my initials since it's my personal blog"; monogram: "let's go with D"; ground motif: "let's go with A. I think it captures well the clean, zen aspect".

SIGNATURE INTERACTION AND MOTION: Nothing animates on load or on page change. The full stop of the monogram is loose. With the pointer nearby it bobs, as an invitation; pressed, it is picked up, and on release it is thrown. From then on the whole window is the field and the pointer carries a bar: every kick off the bar is counted where the stop used to sit, from nought, and the bounce is regular so it can be kept up. The bottom of the window is the only miss: the stop flies home and the match is over until it is picked up again. Steering it back into its place in the monogram banks the run, and a stop that reaches the top of the window makes the hills swell. A hint at the bottom of the window says how to stop; left alone, the stop flies home. On a touch screen the same game starts from the full stop that closes a post: tap to send it up, tap again to keep it there. The hills slide past one another as the top of the page scrolls away, and a click on them raises a swell; they do not follow the pointer. Typing "zen" leaves only the sheet. Nothing moves unless the visitor started it.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
